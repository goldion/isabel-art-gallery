# Idea 1 — Queen Team-Up (pond jumper)

A short **2D lily-pad jumper**, not a click-through. You pick three friends, hop the pond, join the mermaid, beat Gugu, then the show turns into a bubble bath.

Built in `D:\Workspace\god-sisters-world` (see `docs/01-queen-team-up.md` there too).

## Feel

Isabel’s pond page: a **full-size pond**, lily pads, a mermaid who says join our team — **YAY!** Then a cancelled show (mascara pen) and a water-gun / bubble-bath plan.

You **move**. You can fall in. You can retry.

## Play as

Pick **three** of: mermaid, sea queen, sky queen, plant queen, dancing queen, teddy.

The first pick is who you jump with. The other two are extra lives.

## Controls

← → walk. Space / ↑ jump. 1 / 2 / 3 = air / water / plant in the Gugu fight.

## Loop

Jump pads on `full-pond.png` → mermaid **YAY!** → Gugu (fire; water or a stomp beats him) → show cancelled → `bubble-bath.png` **The end!**

## What this is not

Not a list of world buttons. Not a visual novel with **▸**.
